Pillars of Design

The 4F Philosophy

We govern our garment development cycle by four fundamental standards, ensuring every piece you purchase meets structural and visual perfection.

01

Fit

Precision silhouetting, drop-shoulder contours, and engineered seam drops ensure the garment drapes naturally on the human form.

02

Fabric

Heavyweight French Terry loopback cottons, premium long-staple cotton fibers, and organic blends that soften with age.

03

Finish

Reinforced double-needle topstitching, flatlock seam finishes, and garment-washed textures to eliminate pre-shrinkage.

04

Function

Versatile apparel suited for urban exploration or everyday luxury, balancing heavy insulation with breathable structures.

Production Process

The Journey of Creation

Slow fashion requires slow manufacturing. Here is how we develop a silhouette from a draft sketch to a premium finished garment.

1
Stage 01

Pattern Drafting

Every collection begins as a geometric sketch. We calculate drape drop-shoulder values, seam curves, and neckband rib proportions mathematically.

2
Stage 02

Yarn Weaving

Cotton fibers are spun and knit on specialized high-gauge circular looms. Fabric is pre-shrunk before cutting to ensure reliable fit stability.

3
Stage 03

Manual Cutting & Sewing

Fabrics are cut in small batches by master tailors. Assembly is completed using heavy-duty flatlock stitching which creates smooth, durable seams.

4
Stage 04

Stonewash & Inspection

Finished garments are stone-washed to relax fiber tension, resulting in a soft, broken-in hand-feel. Finally, every seam is manually checked.
